Since May I merged a little under 500 pull requests with coding agents. Typing was never the bottleneck.

Now I write constraints instead of code, a separate agent reviews the builder's work, pull requests stay small, and I ask for a screenshot instead of "tests pass".

One thing stayed the same. Someone still has to know what good work looks like.
