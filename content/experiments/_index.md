Every article on this blog gets a small generative piece instead of a stock photo. Each one draws its subject: a CI job moving through its stages, reads hitting or missing cache lines, an agent calling its tools. They are plain canvas and JavaScript, they only animate while on screen, and they hold still if you prefer reduced motion.
